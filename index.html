Good to know

Questions, answered

How long does an order take?

Most personalised pieces are ready within a few days, and larger sets take a little longer. Message us with your date and we will confirm exactly when it will reach you.

Can I add our names and nikkah date?

Yes, that is the heart of what we do. Names, dates, duas and short messages can all be added, and we send you a preview before making anything.

Do you deliver outside my city?

Yes, we deliver across Pakistan. Everything is packed carefully so it arrives exactly as it left us.

Can I order a custom hamper on a budget?

Of course. Tell us your budget and the occasion, and we will put together options that fit.

How do I place an order?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or Instagram with the piece you like. We confirm the details, share a preview, and begin once you approve.
